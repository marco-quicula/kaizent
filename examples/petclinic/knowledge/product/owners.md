# Owners

An owner is a person who brings pets to the clinic.

## Owner fields and rules

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| First Name | Required, up to 30 characters |
| Last Name | Required, up to 30 characters |
| Address | Required, up to 255 characters |
| City | Required, up to 80 characters |
| Telephone | Required, **exactly 10 digits**, with no spaces, dashes, parentheses or `+` (see KI-001) |

When a field breaks a rule, the form shows the message under the field and the owner is not
saved. For the telephone, the message is "Telephone must be a 10-digit number".

## Find owners

1. Click **Find Owners** in the menu.
2. Type the beginning of the owner's **Last Name**, or leave it empty.
3. Click **Find Owner**.

- To **list all owners**, leave **Last Name** empty and click **Find Owner**.
- The results show 5 owners per page; use the page links below the table to see more.
- If exactly one owner matches, PetClinic opens that owner's page directly.
- If no owner matches, the form shows "has not been found" under **Last Name**.

## Add an owner

1. Click **Find Owners**, then **Add Owner**.
2. Fill in all the fields and click **Add Owner**.
3. The owner's page opens with the message "New Owner Created".

## Edit an owner (name, address, city or telephone)

1. Find the owner and open the owner's page.
2. Click **Edit Owner**.
3. Change the fields, for example the **Address** and **City**, and click **Update Owner**.
4. The owner's page opens with the message "Owner Values Updated".

Owners cannot be deleted from the application.
