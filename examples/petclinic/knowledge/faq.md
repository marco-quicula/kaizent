# Frequently asked questions

## How do I see all the owners?

Click **Find Owners**, leave **Last Name** empty and click **Find Owner**. See
[product/owners.md](product/owners.md).

## Which telephone format does PetClinic accept?

Exactly 10 digits, with nothing else: for example `4799990000`. See
[known-issues/KI-001-telephone-validation.md](known-issues/KI-001-telephone-validation.md).

## Can I delete an owner, a pet or a visit?

No. PetClinic has no delete actions. Visits also cannot be cancelled or rescheduled; see
[product/visits.md](product/visits.md).

## Is there a login or user permissions?

No. Everyone who can open PetClinic can see and change all data.

## Where do I report a bug?

Contact the support team with the steps, the values you entered, and the message or
screenshot you saw.
