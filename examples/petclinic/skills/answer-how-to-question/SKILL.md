# Answer a how-to question

1. Identify the task the requester wants to do, in their words and in the product's terms
   (for example, "change an address" means editing an owner).
2. Search the knowledge base for the procedure: the product guides first, then the FAQ.
3. If the procedure exists, answer with numbered steps that use the exact labels of the
   screens and buttons, as written in the knowledge base. Keep it to the steps needed.
4. If the product cannot do what is asked, say so plainly and, when the knowledge base gives
   one, offer the alternative. Never describe a button, menu or setting that the knowledge
   base does not mention.
5. Cite the documents you used.
